Daniel Crowley

Founder & Director

Daniel is the director of Blackwater Digital. He holds a Bachelor of Honors in Construction Management, and his career began in San Francisco, where he worked as a Project Manager and Estimator, gaining experience managing projects and estimating costs. As director, Daniel is the face of Blackwater Digital, representing the company publicly and setting its overall direction. His background in construction management and project estimation gave him a grounding in managing complex, high stakes work, and he brings that same discipline to steering the agency forward.

Bharath_sidde

Project Manager

Bharath is the project manager at Blackwater Digital, overseeing the day to day running of client projects across the agency. With a strong foundation in business strategy and finance, Bharath is responsible for keeping projects on track and ensuring client work is delivered to a high standard. His role focuses on planning, resource management and optimizing internal processes to keep delivery efficient. Bharath's expertise in business strategy and financial management has been central to how Blackwater Digital runs projects and manages client relationships

Sohan Ahammad

Lead Web Designer

Sohan is the lead web developer at Blackwater Digital, coming from a tech background with a specialisation in front end and back end development. He works across multiple platforms, which gives him the flexibility to build the right solution for a client rather than fitting every project into a single system. Sohan leads the technical build process from structure through to launch, balancing front end design with the backend systems that keep a site running reliably. His cross platform experience means Blackwater Digital is not tied to one technology, and clients get a build suited to their actual needs rather than a default template.

Jamie Lee

Marketing Executive

Jamie is a marketing executive at Blackwater Digital, where he specialises in content, social media and broader marketing strategy. He holds a BA International degree, bringing a wider perspective on language and communication into how he shapes campaigns for clients. Jamie builds content that matches each client's voice, runs social strategy that stays consistent across platforms, and shapes the wider marketing approach behind client accounts. His focus is on connecting day to day content work with the bigger strategic goal, so campaigns move with a clear direction rather than just staying active.
