01Is this really 50% off, or is there a catch?

It's a straight 50% off our normal prices: €1,500 becomes €750, €2,500 becomes €1,250 and €5,000 becomes €2,500, all ex VAT. The only limit is the number: 25 websites, first come, first served.

02Why only 25?

Because every site is built by our own team, and 25 is what we can build properly and on time. We'd rather cap the offer than rush the work.

03What happens when the 25 are gone?

The offer closes when the 30th place is claimed or at 11:59pm on Monday 30 November, whichever comes first, and prices go back to normal. The counter on this page shows how many places are left.

04How do I secure my place?

Choose your option and send the claim form on this page. We'll email you an invoice with our bank details. Your place is confirmed the day your payment reaches our bank account, and places are given in the order payments arrive.

05What will it cost with VAT?

VAT at 23% is added to the prices shown: €922.50 for Option 1, €1,537.50 for Option 2 and €3,075 for Option 3.

06Can you really build a website in 5 working days?

Yes. Option 1 takes 5 working days and Option 2 takes 7, counted from the day your payment reaches our bank account. E-commerce sites take 15–20 working days, depending on the number of products.

07Why not just use AI or a DIY website builder?

You can, and for some people that's the right call. But a site that nobody finds, or that doesn't turn visitors into enquiries, costs you more in lost business than it saved. We build sites to bring in customers, and we do the work for you.

08What do I need to give you?

Very little. Logo design, written content and images are all included, so we only need the basics about your business and what you want the site to do.

09I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. Redesigns are covered by the same three options and the same half-price offer.

10Are hosting and maintenance included?

Yes. Hosting and 1 year of maintenance are included in the price.

11How many changes can I ask for?

Option 1 includes 2 rounds of changes, Option 2 includes 4, and Option 3 includes unlimited changes.

12Can you help me get traffic once the site is live?

Yes. We also manage SEO and Google Ads for Irish businesses. That's separate from this offer, and we're happy to talk it through once your site is live.
