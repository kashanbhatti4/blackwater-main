The Overview

A traditional pub with a modern digital front door.

The Blackbird is a traditional pub in the fishing village of Ballycotton, East Cork, known for live music, its beer garden and seafood from the Field Kitchen. Almost everything the pub does changes week to week: who's playing, what's on the menu, which room is booked. The old site couldn't keep up.

We redesigned it around the questions people actually ask a pub: what's on, when can I eat, and can I book it for a party. The answers now sit on the homepage, kept up to date by the team themselves.

Before & after

A pub's website should feel like walking in.

The new homepage opens the way the pub does: bar, food, live music, beer garden, private parties, dogs welcome. It's one line, read in a second, followed straight away by what's on this week. Drag the handle to compare.

What's on

Live music, front and centre.

Music is why many people make the trip to Ballycotton. We built an events listing the bar staff can update in minutes: date, time, act and type. Each entry appears in date order on the homepage and drops off automatically once the night has passed.

Rooms & occasions

Every room sold as a place to celebrate.

Private parties are some of the pub's most valuable bookings: wedding afters, birthdays, christenings, Christmas parties. Each room now has its own card with a photo, a one-line description and a direct enquiry button, so an event organiser can pick a space without having to ring first.
