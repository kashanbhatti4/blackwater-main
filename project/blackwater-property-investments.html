01 / The Brief

One founder, three businesses, and a very specific investment model.

Daniel Crowley started in construction at 13, working alongside his father. What began as Crowley Management rebranded in 2023 into Blackwater Property Investments — a company that buys derelict Cork properties, renovates them, and either sells them on or manages them as rental accommodation for investors, alongside a general contracting arm doing bathroom renovations, extensions and home improvements for homeowners directly.

It's the same trade skill and the same team behind Cork Demolitions, folded into one broader offering here. The site had to hold an investor evaluating a rental-yield projection and a homeowner wanting a bathroom redone in two weeks, without either one feeling like they'd landed on the wrong site.

03 / The Challenge

An investor wants a return. A homeowner wants their bathroom back by Friday week.

The property investment side needed to answer questions no homeowner asks — how properties are sourced, how renovation costs and grants factor into gross development value, how tenants are managed long-term, what returns look like projected over 30 years. The renovation side needed the opposite: a clear, fast answer to "how much, how long, what's included."

The build separates the two into distinct paths from the main navigation — Services for direct renovation work, a dedicated Property Investment page and FAQ for the investor journey — while real, individually detailed case studies (actual project values, grant amounts, timelines) work as shared proof across both audiences.

01 / Structure

Two audiences, two navigation paths
