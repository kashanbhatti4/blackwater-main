The Overview

Architectural Glass & Doors

A full redesign taking Frameless from a brochure site to an online shop, with a bespoke build, a 3D animated hero, SEO, social content and ongoing maintenance.

Earned traffic is up 363% year on year. Frameless now ranks #1 for its own name, appears for 91 keywords, and is the first supplier listed in Google's AI answer for internal sliding doors in Cork.

The brief

From a product description to an online sale.

Frameless had a brochure site. It described the product, listed the range and asked people to get in touch. Everything after that happened by phone, by email, or not at all.

The job was to turn it into a shop without losing what made the product worth buying. Architectural glass is a considered purchase. It's also intensely visual, and almost impossible to sell from a static photograph.

The challenge

A shop is a different animal to a brochure.

Going from brochure to e-commerce isn't a redesign with a checkout bolted on. Product data has to be structured, options have to make sense to someone who isn't a glazier, delivery and lead times have to be stated plainly, and every page that used to end in "contact us" has to end somewhere better.

We rebuilt the site bespoke rather than dropping the range into a theme, because this range doesn't behave like a normal catalogue and a template would have forced it to.

What we did

Build, optimise, show it moving, maintain.

Build

Bespoke, with a 3D hero

A full redesign and custom build that opens on a 3D animated sequence. Glass doesn't look like much in a still photo. It needs light moving across it before it looks worth paying for.

Optimise

Search mapped to real demand

Keyword optimisation on every page of the range, so each product and product type can rank on its own rather than everything funnelling through the homepage. The same clarity is what gets Frameless quoted in AI answers.

Show it moving

Social, because the product is visual

Short-form video on Instagram and Facebook, showing installations and mechanisms in motion.

Maintain

Live site maintenance

Maintenance, updates and support since launch, alongside the marketing, so the shop keeps working instead of slowly falling apart after handover.

Mobile & social

The product moves, so the content moves.

The 3D hero carries to mobile at full quality. It isn't dropped or swapped for a still on small screens, because it's the thing that sells the product. Product images, options and checkout were all built for portrait first.

Short vertical videos on Instagram and Facebook show installations, mechanisms, finishes and the moment a frameless door actually swings. Organic social sessions to the site grew 5.7× year on year.
