01 / The Brief

A boutique Cork brokerage, selling trust before it sells anything else.

Fortis Financial Management is a Cork City brokerage advising high earners and business owners on retirement, protection, investment and succession — regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland, working across products from providers including Irish Life, Zurich, New Ireland and Standard Life.

Nobody books a consultation with a financial advisor on impulse. Before anyone fills in a form, the site has to do the work a first meeting would normally do: establish that this is a regulated, credentialed, independent advisor worth trusting with a pension or a family's protection.

03 / The Challenge

Nine services that sound like each other on paper, and have to read differently on the page.

Retirement Planning, Pension Auto Enrolment, Shareholder Protection, Keyman Insurance, Succession Planning — to a business owner outside the industry, several of these sound interchangeable, and a site that treats them that way undersells the actual breadth of what Fortis offers.

Each service needed its own page, written to its own audience — a business owner insuring against losing a key employee reads a different page to a retiree deciding how to draw down a pension — with the credibility signals (regulation, provider partnerships, credentials) doing consistent work across all of them rather than living in one "About" page nobody reaches.
