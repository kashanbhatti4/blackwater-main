Questions.

(The short answers.)

Is the budget planner free?

Yes, completely. There's no sign-up and no email address needed: answer the questions and your result appears straight away.

How is the budget split worked out?

Your answers about your business stage, your customers and your current spend are weighted into a readiness score and a split across channels. It's a well-informed starting point, not a quote: every business has details a twelve-question tool can't see.

Do you store my answers?

No. The planner runs in your browser and your answers aren't sent to us. If you want us to see them, save the PDF and bring it to a call.

Can I save my results?

Yes. On the results screen, choose Save as PDF to download a one-page report with your score, your budget split and the recommendation.

What should I do with the result?

Start with the fixes it lists first. If you'd like a second opinion, book a free 15-minute call and we'll go through your result with you.
